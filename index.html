Our Mission

The IAA Alumni Association is the official network of the Institute for Advanced Analytics with a mission to promote alumni engagement among all alumni classes. The IAA Alumni Association is led by a volunteer IAA Alumni Board that also coordinates the regional IAA Alumni Council to support involvement across the country.
